- **30%** Deep Forest/Sage (typography, UI elements)

## 🔤 Typography

### Primary Font: **Inter** (Free on Google Fonts)
Clean, modern, highly legible — perfect for healthcare DTC

| Element | Weight | Size | Tracking |
|---------|--------|------|----------|
| Headlines | Bold (700) | 32-48px | -1% |
| Subheadlines | Semi-Bold (600) | 24-28px | 0% |
| Body Copy | Regular (400) | 16-18px | 0% |
| Captions | Medium (500) | 14px | +1% |
| CTAs | Bold (700) | 16-20px | +2% |

### Alternative Fonts
- **Headlines Alternative:** Libre Franklin (bolder, more editorial)
- **Accent/Quotes:** Playfair Display (sophisticated moments only)

### Typography Rules
1. **Maximum 2 fonts per graphic** — usually just Inter
2. **Left-align text** — never center long paragraphs
3. **Line height:** 1.4x for body, 1.1x for headlines
4. **Avoid ALL CAPS** except for very short CTAs (2-3 words max)

## 📐 Image Composition Guidelines

### The Hims/Ro Formula
```
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                     │
│     40% Breathing Room / Sky        │
│                                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│                                     │
│     35% Hero Product/Person         │
│     (Rule of thirds placement)      │
│                                     │
├─────────────────────────────────────┤
│     25% Surface/Ground              │
│     (Subtle shadow, texture)        │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Whitespace Ratios
- **Minimum 30% whitespace** on every graphic
- **Product shots:** 50%+ whitespace
- **Lifestyle shots:** 25-35% whitespace
- **Text overlays:** Never cover more than 40% of image

### Product Photography Guidelines

#### Hero Shots
- **Angle:** Slight 3/4 view (not flat frontal)
- **Shadow:** Soft, diffused, 45° angle
- **Background:** Solid cream `#FAF9F6` or soft gradient
- **Props:** Minimal — botanical elements, water droplets, clean surfaces

#### Lifestyle Integration
- **Models:** Diverse, 25-45 age range, natural expressions
- **Setting:** Bright, airy bathrooms/bedrooms, neutral tones
- **Interaction:** Natural product use, not posed holding
- **Lighting:** Soft natural light, no harsh shadows

### Composition Rules
1. **Rule of thirds** — product at intersection points
2. **Single focal point** — one hero element per image
3. **Visual hierarchy:** Product → Text → Logo
4. **Negative space direction** — leave room where eye travels

## ✍️ Caption Formulas That Convert

### Formula 1: Problem-Agitate-Solution (PAS)
```
[Emoji] [Relatable problem statement]

[1-2 sentences agitating the pain point]

[Solution introduction with Anagen]

[Social proof or science callout]

[CTA] 👇
```

**Example:**
```
🪞 Noticed your hairline looking different lately?

You're not imagining it. 67% of men experience visible 
thinning by 35 — and the earlier you act, the better 
your results.

Anagen's clinically-backed formula works with your 
body's natural hair cycle, not against it.

Join 10,000+ members seeing real results.

Link in bio to start your journey 👇
```

### Formula 2: Social Proof Lead
```
[Quote or stat] 📊

[Brief context]

[Product tie-in]

[Soft CTA]
```

**Example:**
```
"I wish I'd started 2 years ago" — Jake, 34 📊

We hear this constantly. Hair loss is progressive, 
which means the best time to act is now.

Anagen members see visible improvement in as little 
as 3-4 months.

Tap the link to see what's possible ✨
```

### Formula 3: Education-First
```
💡 [Did you know / Quick fact headline]

[2-3 educational sentences]

[How Anagen addresses this]

[Question to drive engagement]
```

**Example:**
```
💡 DHT is the #1 cause of male pattern hair loss

This hormone shrinks follicles over time until they 
stop producing visible hair entirely.

Anagen's formula targets DHT at the source while 
supporting follicle health from within.

What questions do you have about how it works? 
Drop them below 👇
```

### Formula 4: Before/After Storytelling
```
[Time marker] ➡️ [Transformation]

[Brief story in 2-3 lines]

[Encouraging message]

[CTA]
```

### Caption Writing Rules
1. **First line is everything** — make it scroll-stopping
2. **Line breaks after every 1-2 sentences** — scannable
3. **Emojis:** 2-4 per caption, never consecutive
4. **Length:** 125-220 words optimal for engagement
5. **Voice:** Confident but not aggressive, empathetic not preachy
6. **Avoid:** "Buy now!", excessive exclamation points, medical claims

- **Minimum:** 4 posts/week

- **Stories:** 3-7 per day
- **Reels:** 3-5 per week

### Content Mix Ratios
- **40%** Educational/Value content
- **25%** Social proof/Results
- **20%** Lifestyle/Relatable
- **15%** Direct product/CTA

## ✅ Quick Reference Checklist

Before posting, verify:

### Visual Check
- [ ] Minimum 30% whitespace
- [ ] Product clearly visible and hero
- [ ] Colors from approved palette only
- [ ] Text is legible (contrast check)
- [ ] Logo placement subtle (bottom corner)
- [ ] No busy backgrounds

### Copy Check
- [ ] Hook in first line
- [ ] Proper line breaks
- [ ] 2-4 emojis only
- [ ] Clear CTA included
- [ ] No medical claims
- [ ] Brand voice: confident + empathetic

### Technical Check
- [ ] Correct dimensions (1080x1080 or 1080x1350)
- [ ] High resolution (no pixelation)
- [ ] Alt text added
- [ ] Hashtags in first comment (feed posts)
- [ ] Location tag if relevant

## 🚫 Brand Don'ts

1. **Never use** red, bright yellow, or neon colors
2. **Never show** dramatic hair loss imagery (be tasteful)
3. **Never make** medical claims or guarantees
4. **Never use** aggressive sales language ("ACT NOW!")
5. **Never post** without proper whitespace
6. **Never use** stock photos with watermarks
7. **Never compare** directly to competitors by name
8. **Never use** before/after without consent + disclaimer
